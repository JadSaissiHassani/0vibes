# 0Vibes
I built this because selling stuff online shouldn't be complicated. Not everyone wants to spend hours setting up an entire e-commerce website just to sell a few products.
0Vibes is a website where people can publish their products and sell them online without all the unnecessary hassle.
Create your listings, show people what you're selling, and get your stuff out there. That's the idea.





## What it does
0Vibes makes it easier for people to put their products online and start selling.

Publish products — list the things you want to sell without building your own website
Showcase your stuff — give your products a place where people can discover them
Reach potential buyers — make it easier for people to find what you're selling
Keep it simple — focus on your products instead of dealing with complicated website setups
The goal is to make selling online accessible to everyone, not just people who know how to build websites.





## Why 0Vibes?
Want to sell something online? Usually, you have to choose between posting on social media, dealing with complicated marketplaces, or building your own store from scratch.
None of that should be a requirement just to get your products in front of people.
0Vibes is built around a simple idea: anyone should be able to publish what they're selling and give it a chance to find a buyer.
Whether you're selling products you've made yourself, things from a small business, or items from your own collection, getting started should be easy.





## The idea
A simple place to bring sellers and buyers together.
No need to build an entire e-commerce website from scratch. Just publish your products and let people discover what you have to offer.
The focus is on making the experience straightforward, accessible, and easy to use.





## Who is it for?

Small businesses looking for a simple way to showcase their products
Independent creators who want to sell things they've made
Entrepreneurs testing a new product or business idea
Anyone who has something to sell and wants to put it online
